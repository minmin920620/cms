Crime Incident Recording Process    `n`n              The Crime Incident Recording Process begins when the complainant reports a crime incident to the Desk Officer through a walk-in complaint. The Desk Officer receives and assesses the report, then forwards it to the Duty Investigator. The Duty Investigator manually fills out the CIRAS form and reviews the details with the complainant. Once the information is verified, the complainant signs the IRF, after which the Desk Officer logs the record and the Crime Registrar compiles it for UCPER.   `n`n                                                 `n`n                 Figure           2          .
